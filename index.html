Why Choose Us

Your Trusted Lab Partner Since 2002

Sunset Dental Prosthetics Laboratory is owned and operated by a husband-and-wife team, Tatyana and Gennady Rabovsky, both Certified Dental Technicians with Master's Degrees in Dental Technology. With over 30 years of experience, we've built a reputation for excellence, precision, and consistency.

Unlike larger labs where work is assigned to different technicians, we personally craft every restoration ourselves — ensuring consistent quality, fit, and aesthetics for every case. For milled restorations, we personally adjust all contacts, occlusion, and excursive movements on the model, ensuring minimal chair-side adjustment for the dentist.
